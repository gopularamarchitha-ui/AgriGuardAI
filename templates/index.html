      General Crop Disease Prevention Guidelines

      Essential preventive practices recommended for smallholder farmers:

      • Sanitation (పరిశుభ్రత): Remove and safely bury infected foliage to prevent spore spread.
      • Plant Spacing (దూరం): Maintain spacing between plants for adequate canopy airflow.
      • Watering Management (నీటి యాజమాన్యం): Avoid late afternoon overhead watering. Water near soil base.
      • Field Scouting (పరిశీలన): Inspect leaf undersides weekly for early symptom spots.
      • Seed Quality (విత్తన నాణ్యత): Always use certified disease-free planting seeds.
      • Crop Rotation (పంట మార్పిడి): Practice multi-year crop rotation with non-host crops.
